At Reynolds Dryline, we recognise that the construction industry has a significant role to play in addressing the climate challenge. That's why we are continuously investing in new techniques, materials, and processes that reduce the environmental impact of our work.

## Sustainable Insulation Solutions

We have been working with our supply chain partners to introduce a range of sustainable insulation products that deliver exceptional thermal and acoustic performance while significantly reducing embodied carbon. These include recycled mineral wool products, bio-based insulation materials, and high-performance rigid board systems.

## Reducing Waste on Site

We have implemented a comprehensive waste management programme across all our sites, with the goal of diverting at least 90% of construction waste from landfill. This includes careful material planning to minimise offcuts, on-site segregation of waste streams, and partnerships with specialist recycling facilities.

## Committed to a Greener Future

Our commitment to sustainability is not just about compliance — it's about doing the right thing for future generations. We will continue to invest in sustainable practices and work with our clients and supply chain partners to drive positive change across the sector.
