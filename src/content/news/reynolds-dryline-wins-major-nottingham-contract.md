Reynolds Dryline is delighted to announce the award of a major contract to deliver the drylining and building finishes package for the Bendigo Building development in Nottingham city centre.

## About the Project

The Bendigo Building is a significant residential development in the heart of Nottingham, comprising hundreds of high-quality apartments. Reynolds Dryline has been appointed by main contractor Graham to deliver the complete internal building finishes package, including metal stud partitioning, suspended ceilings, and structural framing systems.

## Our Scope of Works

Our scope on this project includes the installation of metal stud partitioning systems throughout the building, suspended ceiling systems in common areas and apartments, structural framing systems (SFS) for the external envelope, and acoustic and fire-rated systems to meet the project's demanding performance requirements.

## A Strong Partnership

We are proud to be working with Graham on this prestigious project. Our teams have worked together on previous developments, and we look forward to building on that strong working relationship to deliver an exceptional result for the client.
